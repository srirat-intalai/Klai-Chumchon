# Surface brief: Klai Chumchon web app (all routes)

Scope: whole app shell and every route under client/src. Visitor mode: Operate (report, track, triage). Home hero is the only Persuade-leaning moment.

Audience and job: residents report a problem from a phone at the spot and track it; staff triage and change status; admin manages. Constraints: Thai UI, all current functions and states kept, status colours red/amber/green keep their meaning everywhere, dark mode kept.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A plum stage frames a calm bone workspace; the community's problems read as one shared, honest ledger. Refuses the category default of a grey dashboard with a green civic accent.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned Toggl-style system. Plum #2c1338 header, hero band and footer; Bone #fefbfa canvas and cards; Heather #412a4c headings; Lavender Smoke/Dusty Mauve body; Silver #d5d0d7 hairlines; Blush/Bubblegum pills; one Magenta #e57cd8 element per screen (primary CTA or headline emphasis word). Flat, no shadows; radii 10px cards/inputs, 26px buttons, 200px pills. Prompt display (Thai-capable geometric stand-in for GT Haptik), Inter + Noto Sans Thai body.

STORY: Visitor sees the shared map and live counts, understands status at a glance, reports in minutes, returns to see who changed what.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plum band holds the headline with one Magenta italic emphasis phrase, subcopy, Magenta "แจ้งปัญหาใหม่" CTA plus ghost map button, and the live map card at right; status counts sit on the band as pills.

FORM: Pinned whole by the user (design system files supplied). Standard app shell and controls; world enters through palette, type, density and the signature move. seed: none (pinned)

SIGNATURE: The single Magenta italic emphasis word in each page headline on the plum hero, and status shown as tinted pills consistent with map pins.
